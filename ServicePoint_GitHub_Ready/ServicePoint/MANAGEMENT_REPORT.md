## Findings
1. Billing and technical faults account for 419 cases (58.2%). Their repeat rates are 38.1% and 36.8% respectively.
2. Technical faults have the longest average initial resolution time (32.48 hours). Billing averages 29.77 hours.
3. August and September phone/chat wait averages are 10.93 and 10.71 minutes. Arrival timing and staffing data are needed to explain this pattern.
4. October has the highest average initial resolution time (30.11 hours). Review case mix and handoffs before choosing an intervention.
5. Satisfaction averages 3.91/5, but 164 cases have no score. Do not treat missing scores as zero.

## Pilot evaluation
Collect four baseline weeks, then four pilot weeks using unchanged definitions. Compare similar issue mixes and show group sizes. Evaluate FCR, repeat case rate, complaints and CSAT together. Set improvement targets with the fictional business owner; this study does not impose an unsupported industry benchmark. A before/after change alone would not prove causality. Check staffing changes, survey coverage and other operational changes.

## Limitations
All records and patterns are synthetic. The current dataset contains no direct root-cause evidence, measured intervention or realised savings. Proposed process maps are assumptions and designs, not observed workflows. Team mapping is tied to contact reason. Do not generalise results to a real employer.
