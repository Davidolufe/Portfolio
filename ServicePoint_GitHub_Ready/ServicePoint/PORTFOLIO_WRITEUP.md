# ServicePoint portfolio description — draft to adapt after study

ServicePoint is an AI-assisted portfolio case study analysing 720 synthetic broadband customer-service cases from May to October 2026. It explores contact demand, initial resolution time, seven-day repeat contact, complaints and satisfaction.

The project includes an Excel analysis workbook, an offline interactive dashboard, SQL setup and analysis scripts, and proposed AS-IS/TO-BE process maps. Confirmed first-contact resolution requires an initial resolution and no repeat contact within seven days. Missing survey scores and email wait times are excluded from the relevant averages.

The synthetic analysis shows billing and technical faults contribute 58.2% of cases and have repeat rates of 38.1% and 36.8%. Recommendations include clearer billing guidance, a technical diagnostic checklist and named ownership of unresolved cases. These are proposed interventions; no real-world savings or improvements have been measured.
